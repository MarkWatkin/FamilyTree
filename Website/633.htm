Sylvester DOYLE1, 6th child of Thomas DOYLE and Marcella CLYNCH, was born on 17/11/1882 in Corragh, Clonegal, Carlow, Ireland. Sylvester died on 01/07/1960 in Ballinea, Mullingar, Westmeath, Ireland aged 77.

Mary Anne Ellen DUNNE2, eldest daughter of Patrick DUNNE and Sarah CASSIDY, was born on 26/02/1900 in 7 Derrymore, Killucan, Westmeath, Ireland. She died on 01/09/1970 aged 70.

On 05/10/1934 Sylvester married Mary. 2 sons and one daughter given.
