Mary Ann REDDICAN, eldest child of John REDDICAN and Mary QUEENAN [Family], was born about 1853 in Sheffield, Yorkshire, England.

Lived at 76 Brown Street with parents but in 1871 by the age of 29 her father had died and she has left home. In 1881 she is back with her mother in 78 Brown Street, with John and an uncle Martin from Boyle Ireland (perhaps John Reddican's brother).

No children listed.
