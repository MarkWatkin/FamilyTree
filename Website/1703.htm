Notes

1. Prior to getting married Maria lived in Low Town, Worksop, Nottinghamshire and was listed as a servant. After marrying William Hallam from nearby Bilby they moved between Worksop and Bilby and raised a family. In 1871 they appear to be living with or next door to the Mallender family (where John Mallender is also from Barnby and Mary Mallender is also from Worksop). By 1881 they have moved to 5 Bracebridge, with Maria and youngest son Walter. By 1891 (still at 5 Bracebridge) Walters has left home but now they have granddaughter Maria Mallender living with them.; [Note Record]
