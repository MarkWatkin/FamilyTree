Notes

1. John Winter Richardson was born at the family farm, Trehowel in Weston Rhyn/St Martins. By the time he was 15 in 1881, his family moved to the larger Tyn-y-Celyn farm. He married Sarah Ann Rogers around 1893 and by 1901 he was farming Escob Farm (between St Martins and Weston Rhyn) where he had 2 servants. By 1911 he had taken over his fathers farm Tyn-y-Celyn, and his widowed father had come to stay with him, where they now had 3 servants. By 1912 he was still at Tyn-y-Celyn according to the Kelly's directory of businesses. It is unclear whether or not he and the other Richardson's owned these lands or were tenant farmers. His sister Charlotte died in 1927 and for some reason she left £280 7s 6d to him, even though he was a successful farmer and Charlotte left a husband and family behind (This amount would have been roughly equivalent to the price of Charlotte's family home at the time); [Note Record]
