Notes

1. The Aldous family name can be traced back to the year 1450 when a William Aldous was christened in the Suffolk town of Fressingfield. We are not aware of any records as to who were William Aldous's Parents but it is on record that other Aldous's of the same generation lived in Moulton and Huntingfield. William Aldous married a Johane WARNER around 1477. Johana was christened 1456 in Wingfield and was the daughter of Thomas Warner, son of a Nicholas Warner. William owned land in Fressingfield and the district parish of Chippenhall and was Church Warden at the Parish Church of St. Peter and St.Paul in 1493. William and Johane had three sons, Robert, Richard and Thomas William Aldous died on 15th January 1531 and was buried in Fressingfield. He left a will dated 13th December 1528 but so far this as not been accessed to find what provisions were made for its content which included - Fields in Whittingham; Pightle called Godbaldys in Chippenhall; Four acres in Fressingfield; A field called Ferfeld in Fressingfield; A field called Imphedgeland in Chippenhall.; [Note Record]
2. Thomas grew up in Fressingfield and spent some adult years in nearby Wingfield. Deeds give Wingfield as his residence in 1452 and 1478, Fressingfield in 1502 and 1503. A Thomas Warner of Wingfield prepared his will 24 Feb 1504, naming daughter Alice and son John. This Thomas could be Johane's father, if he omitted her from his will because she had already received her portion although further evidence is, however, required.; [Note Record]
3. Nicholas Warner, a carpenter, born in Fressingfield, Suffolk, England, and died there in 1477. Fressingfield deeds include: - 18 Dec 1427, John Levald gave Nicholas Warner, John Fox, and William Delanges of Fressingfield 4 acres of land in Fressingfield - 31 Jul 1429, Robert Noyes, William Warner of Fressingfield, John ...let of Dickelborough, and John Jay of Nedham gave Nicholas Warner of Fressingfield a messuage (house, outbuildings, and yard) etc. containing three roods (quarter acre) - 17 Aug 1442, Thomas Cook of Wingfield gave Nicholas Warner a messuage with a piece of land - 30 Oct 1452, Robert Noyes of Fressingfield gave Nicholas Warner of Fressingfield, Thomas Cook of Wingfield, and Thomas Warner of Wingfield, a messuage, built upon, called Krichotys in Fressingfield. In his will, dated 20 Nov 1476 (probated 14 Jun 1477), Nicholas Warner requested burial in the Fressingfield churchyard. He made bequests to the High Altar of that church and for repairs of the same, to the chapel of the Blessed Margaret, and also to the monastery of Friars Minor, Norwich, for the celebration of 30 masses; the residue he gave to Thomas Warner "my son of Wingfield and James (Jacobo) Cunwald of the same, my executors."; [Note Record]
