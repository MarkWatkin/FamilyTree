Notes

1. Born in Llansilin, Denbighshire, Wales in 1775, in 1838 Edward is listed as a farmer from Bryn on daughter Mary's marriage cert. He lived in Bryn Hall, Llanyblodwel through the 1841 and 1851 census with his wife Mary at a seemingly substantial farm. Here he rears his grand daughter Jane Bywater after his daughter Mary (Jane's mother) has died before Jane's 3rd birthday and son-in-law is not listed as living locally. Oddly his grand daughter Jane is listed as his daughter and niece on 2 census. (Edward was born the same year as Jane Austen); [Note Record]
2. Little is known of Mary, husband of Edward Jones, She was not present from the census for Bryn Hall in 1841, although the was listed there as Edwards wife in 1851 and his widow by 1861; [Note Record]
3. Mary daughter of farmer Edward Jones, presumably with some wealth, marries John Bywater in 1838, has daughter Jane, but then dies young in 1839 leaving her daughter Jane to be raised by her parents Edward and Mary at Bryn Hall, Llanyblodwel; [Note Record]
