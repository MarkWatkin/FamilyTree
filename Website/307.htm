Annie LEWIS, 6th child of George LEWIS and Ann CURRIE [Family], was born between 04/1885 and 1886 in Maesbury Marsh, Oswestry, Shropshire, England. She died on 12/04/1971 in High Ercall, Shropshire, England aged about 86. Annie was buried in 04/1971 in St.Michael's Churchyard High Ercall, Shropshire.

She resided in 1891 in Marsh Road, Maesbury, Shropshire, England. Annie resided in 1901 in St Winifreds Wellhouse, Woolston, Shropshire, England.

Anne was born in Maesbury. She lived at Marsh Road initially but later lived at the famous St Winifred Wellhouse in Woolston with his parents in 1901. She married Percy Harper in the second quarter 1915 at High Ercall after courting for eight years at Cotwall, High Ercall Shropshire where they worked on the farm of Mr. Bruikshaw (according to granddaughter Valerie Harper. She was later brought back to see St Winifred's Wellhouse by her son and was saddened to see it had fallen into disrepair by the local farmer. It has since been refurbished.
