Notes

1. Thomas grew up in Fressingfield and spent some adult years in nearby Wingfield. Deeds give Wingfield as his residence in 1452 and 1478, Fressingfield in 1502 and 1503. A Thomas Warner of Wingfield prepared his will 24 Feb 1504, naming daughter Alice and son John. This Thomas could be Johane's father, if he omitted her from his will because she had already received her portion although further evidence is, however, required.; [Note Record]
2. The Aldous family name can be traced back to the year 1450 when a William Aldous was christened in the Suffolk town of Fressingfield. We are not aware of any records as to who were William Aldous's Parents but it is on record that other Aldous's of the same generation lived in Moulton and Huntingfield. William Aldous married a Johane WARNER around 1477. Johana was christened 1456 in Wingfield and was the daughter of Thomas Warner, son of a Nicholas Warner. William owned land in Fressingfield and the district parish of Chippenhall and was Church Warden at the Parish Church of St. Peter and St.Paul in 1493. William and Johane had three sons, Robert, Richard and Thomas William Aldous died on 15th January 1531 and was buried in Fressingfield. He left a will dated 13th December 1528 but so far this as not been accessed to find what provisions were made for its content which included - Fields in Whittingham; Pightle called Godbaldys in Chippenhall; Four acres in Fressingfield; A field called Ferfeld in Fressingfield; A field called Imphedgeland in Chippenhall.; [Note Record]
3. Robert, the eldest son of William and Johane was born and baptised in Fressingfield during 1479. He married a woman called Elizabeth in 1519 but her maiden surname is not known. They had six children, Joanne, Agnes, William, Thomas, Alice, John and Robert.; [Note Record]
4. Elizabeth died on the 11th February 1575. A will of an Elizabeth Aldous, a widow of Fressingfield, dated 11th April 1566 was witnessed by a Thomas Gowyn. Whether or not this is the same Elizabeth as still to be verified.; [Note Record]
5. William and Margaret resided in Fressingfield, where they had various properties, some or all of which William had inherited from his father Robert, who died in 1560. The inheritance included the family dwelling with adjoining lands, called Bourneys, with a portion of the home reserved for the use of Robert's widow during the remainder of her life. William also received an enclosed field, called Bardenes, and a major portion of a farm, called Cotwyns. Additionally, he inherited his father's horse mill, with stones and other appurtenances; four milk cows, and a bay mare. William's mother lived until 1576, William having provided her with the various items she needed, as stipulated in his father's will.; [Note Record]
6. William and Margaret resided in Fressingfield, where they had various properties, some or all of which William had inherited from his father Robert, who died in 1560.; [Note Record]
7. Stephens will deposited on the 16th June 1622 and probated on the 19th February 1627 included - Lands called Bourneys, Peaslye, Hushawe Green, meassuage called Cotwaynes or Bablons and a meassuage in Whittingham.; [Note Record]
