Walter HARPER, eldest son of Percy HARPER and Annie LEWIS [Family], was born on 07/06/1917 in 3 Cotwall, High Ercall, Shropshire, England. Walter died in 03/1986 in Portsmouth, Hampshire, Ireland aged about 69.

He was a Army Sargent in 1940 in Cairo. He resided in 1986 in 10 Winterhill Road, Cosham, Portsmouth, Hampshire, England.

Walter Harper was born in 3 Cotwall, High Ercall, Shropshire He worked as a farm boy in Shropshire. He was a WW2 Army Sgt.S.I.B (Special Investigation Branch) in Cairo, Code Breaker & Detective. Fireman & Driver + turntable ladder operator till retirement. Then he worked at Barrells the undertakers. In 1986 he dies of a stroke and heart attack while living at 10 Winterhill Rd., Cosham, Portsmouth, Hampshire, England. His ashes interred at St Michael's Churchyard High Ercall, Shropshire with his Mum & Dad, Percy & Annie.
