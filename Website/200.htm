Catherine was born in a house on Seafort Parade, Booterstown in 1896. Her family moved around various houses in the Rock road area as she grew up. She met her husband Arthur in the neighbourhood, as he moved here as a young man with his widowed mother and brothers. She raised 3 children (and a fourth died in infancy) at 21 Rock road. She was widowed young in life and moved to a larger house at 37 Rock road with 2 of her daughters Veronica and Winifred, known as Una.
