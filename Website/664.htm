Percy HARPER was born on 11/11/1891 in High Ercall, Shropshire, England. He died in 1972 in Shropshire, England aged about 81. Percy was buried in 1972 in St.Michael's Churchyard High Ercall, Shropshire.

Annie LEWIS1, 6th child of George LEWIS and Ann CURRIE, was born between 04/1885 and 1886 in Maesbury Marsh, Oswestry, Shropshire, England. She died on 12/04/1971 in High Ercall, Shropshire, England aged about 86. She was buried in 04/1971 in St.Michael's Churchyard High Ercall, Shropshire.

In 1891 Annie resided in Marsh Road, Maesbury, Shropshire, England. She resided in 1901 in St Winifreds Wellhouse, Woolston, Shropshire, England.

In 1915 Percy married Annie. 2 sons and 3 daughters given.

Notes

1. Anne was born in Maesbury. She lived at Marsh Road initially but later lived at the famous St Winifred Wellhouse in Woolston with his parents in 1901.
