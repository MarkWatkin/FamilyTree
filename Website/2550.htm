Notes

1. Elizabeth Winter (signed her name Betsy on all census forms and certs). She was born in Huntingfield, Halesworth in Suffolk of parents Susan and Samuel Winter. I have reason to believe that this Susan is the same Susan that appears in a genealogical study "William D Clutton, Genealogy and family history of William Clutton (1675-1732) of Fressingfield, Suffolk, England". In this book a Susan Clutton marries a Samuel Winter, gardener for Lord Huntingfields estate., and they have a child Elizabeth Winter in Huntingfield. In this book Samuel and Susan move to Farnworth, Bolton near Manchester in 1836. to work on Thomas Barnes cotton mill and Susan dies in 1850. I found an 1851 census record for Farnworth, which lists a Samuel from Chediston near Huntingfield, Suffolk, with a wife Helena Maria (presumably second wife ). They have with them a child Betsy Winter also from Huntingfield. I also found an 1861 census record which puts the same Samuel Winter and Helena Maria in Bronygarth only a few hundred yards from Betsy Winter and James Richardsons farm. Hence my belief that our Betsy Winter's parents are indeed Samuel Winter and Susan Clutton, from the William Clutton book from which the above ancestry all flows. Betsy Winter ends up in Shropshire, possibly due to the fact that her father's new wife comes from nearby Cheshire. She marries James Richardson in 1860. They farm at Trehowel Farm of 30 acres, then Tyn-y-Celyn farm of 117 acres, and employerd servants and labourers. They then moved to another farm, and their 2 sons took over Trehowel and Tyn-y- Celyn both employing staff. Is has been reported that they "disowned" their youngest, Charlotte, when she became pregnant and later married Edward Lewis (who she may have met at the blacksmth in neighbouring St Martins where he was working as an apprentice). Betsy died before her husband at Pentre Bronygarth, Weston Rhyn leaving £136 16s 2d to her husband James.; [Note Record]
