1. Gladys was born of an earlier partner of Mary Helen and raised as an aunt with the Lewis name. Although she did come to live with her siblings a little later on after mother Mary Helen married William Owen. Gladys married in Gobowen and stayed in the village to this day.; [Note Record]
2. Edward Henry, known as Henry in the UK and Harry in Ireland was born in Gobowen, Oswestry. As a young man he spent some time in the RAF and won scholarships to study at University, but decided against such careers. He met his wife Veronica on a tour or Europe in 1954 and moved to Ireland where he raised a large family, working various careers, including managing his own contract cleaning company. He had a passion for carpentry, building dolls houses and was a keen artist specialising in pencil and pen and ink drawings. He retired to West Cork with Veronica and his daughter Judith's family, where he spends much time in the garden.; [Note Record]
