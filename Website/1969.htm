Notes

1. Andrew Currie was born in Stewarton, East Ayrshire in Scotland where he was christened some months later. He moved to Daventry in Northamptonshire in the 1840s where he met his future wife Rebecca Powell Adams. He worked as various jobs over his life (Tea trader, rag and bone man, pedlar, insurance agent). By 1861 he had moved to Shropshire with his family where he lived out the rest of his life. In his 80s he and his wife moved in with his daughter Ann with her husband George Lewis an their children in Munslow Cottages, Maesbury Marsh.; [Note Record]
