Charlotte RICHARDSON, 3rd child of James RICHARDSON and Betsy WINTER [Family], was born on 05/06/1870 in Trehowel, St Martins, Oswestry, Shropshire, England. Charlotte died on 22/11/1927 in Brynycastell Cottage, Gobowen, Oswestry, Shropshire, England aged 57.

She resided on 05/06/1870 in Trehowel, St Martins, Oswestry, Shropshire, England. She resided on 03/04/1881 in Tyn-Y-Celyn, Weston Rhyn, Shropshire, Ireland. On 13/10/1899 Charlotte resided in Maesbury, Oswestry, Shropshire, England. She resided on 31/03/1901 in 40 New Hall View, Ruabon, Shropshire, England. Charlotte resided on 28/03/1902 in Bringewood, Queen Street, Ruabon, Denbigh, Wrexham, Wales. Charlotte resided on 02/04/1911 in 1 Bryn Castle Terrace, Gobowen, Oswestry, Shropshire, England. On 22/11/1927 she resided in Brynycastell Cottage, Gobowen, Oswestry, Shropshire, England.

Charlotte Richardson was born at the family farm, Trehowel in Weston Rhyn/St Martins. By the time she was 10 in 1881, her family moved to the larger Tyn-y-Celyn farm. In 1891 her future husband Edward was probably working as an apprentice blacksmith in the next village of St Martins, and it is probably here that they met. Edward and Charlotte marry in late 1899 in the Oswestry registry office (about 4 months pregnant with their first child Edward Donald). At the time of the marriage they are both living in Maesbury (Marsh) and the marriage is witnessed by Edward's parents. This, and word of mouth evidence suggests that Charlotte's match and/or premarital pregnancy caused friction between her and family and she was "disowned". Although later on Charlotte's grandchildren did visit the Richardson's farm so the "disowning" was not entire. After the marriage by 1901, they lived in 40 New Hall View, Ruabon with new baby Edward Donald and Edward is employed as Railway platelayer (These houses are gone, replaced by St Michael's Close). A year later, Mary Helen is born at Bringewood, Queen Street, Ruabon and Edward is still listed as a platelayer, as he is in 1911 by which time they have moved to 1 Bryn Castle Terrace, Gobowen, just opposite the school. At some point between 1918 and 1927 they moved into Brynycastle Cottage, Gobowen and ultimately passed this house onto daughter Mary Helen, who raised her own family there with William Owen. In 1927, Charlotte died and for some unknown reason leaves £280 7s 6d to her eldest brother, even though he was a successful farmer and Charlotte left a husband and family behind (This amount would have been roughly equivalent to the price of Charlotte's family home at the time).

3 sons and 3 daughters listed.
