Willam Owen WATKIN, 4th child of Robert Henry WATKIN and Margaret Jane DAVIES [Family], was born on 23/07/1903 in Glyn Morlas, St Martins, Shropshire Road, England. Willam died on 01/03/1967 in Oswestry, Shropshire, England aged 63.

He resided on 02/04/1911 in 15 Glyn Morlas, St Martins, Oswestrry, Shropshire, England. He resided on 10/04/1931 in 1 Oswald Place, Oswestry, Shropshire, England. On 11/04/1931 Willam resided in Brynycastell Cottage, Gobowen, Oswestry, Shropshire, England. He was a Described As Builders Labourer on Marriage cert on 11/04/1931.

Born and reared in Glyn Morlas, Oswestry and worked as a carpenter in Gobowen, Oswestry, Shropshire, England. William lived with his family in Brynycastell Cottage where he raised his 3 children.
