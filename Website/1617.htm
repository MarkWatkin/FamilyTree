Notes

1. Veronica grew up in Booterstown on the Rock Road with her 2 sisters. She enjoyed long cycles with her father Arthur around Wicklow and was a great fan of opera music. She met her husband Harry on European tour with her sister in 1954. She settled in Dublin where she raised a family of 8 children with Harry (6 of whom were born at home). She retired with Harry to West Cork with Harry and her daughter, Judith's family, where she enjoys the country life in the village of Skibbereen.; [Note Record]
