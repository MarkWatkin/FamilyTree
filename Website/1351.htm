Notes

1. Thomas was born near Booterstown, and baptised in St John the Baptist Church, Blackrock. Presume he lived at Seafort Parade in 1869 where Catherine was born. His age is reported inconsistently on census forms which also report that they had another child who died in infancy. He lived in various houses in Booterstown. He listed himself as a general labourer. We think Thomas had about 6 brothers including a John.; [Note Record]
2. Bridget was born in Coolmore, Co Wicklow. Seems to have lived in various housed around Booterstown She gave birth to Catherine in a house on Seafort Parade, Blackrock. Census suggests they had a third child who died at infancy. Bridget was remembered as a short lady.; [Note Record]
3. Catherine was born in a house on Seafort Parade, Booterstown in 1896. Her family moved around various houses in the Rock road area as she grew up. She met her husband Arthur in the neighbourhood, as he moved here as a young man with his widowed mother and brothers. She raised 3 children (and a fourth died in infancy) at 21 Rock road. She was widowed young in life and moved to a larger house at 37 Rock road with 2 of her daughters Veronica and Winifred, known as Una.; [Note Record]
4. Lived with parents at 15 Rock Road during 1901 census Lived with parents at 18 Emmet Square during 1911 census Paid for the the burial of a Thomas Doyle at Deansgrange in 1952; [Note Record]
