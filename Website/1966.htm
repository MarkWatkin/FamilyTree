Notes

1. Andrew Currie was born in Stewarton, East Ayrshire in Scotland where he was christened some months later. He moved to Daventry in Northamptonshire in the 1840s where he met his future wife Rebecca Powell Adams. He worked as various jobs over his life (Tea trader, rag and bone man, pedlar, insurance agent). By 1861 he had moved to Shropshire with his family where he lived out the rest of his life. In his 80s he and his wife moved in with his daughter Ann with her husband George Lewis an their children in Munslow Cottages, Maesbury Marsh.; [Note Record]
2. The marriage was witnessed by Francis Hickman and Harriot Lydia Davies; [Note Record]
3. Ann was born in Daventry, Northamptonshire, where her mother grew up and her Scottish father moved to. Prior to her marriage in 1873 she is living at Bromwick Park, Maesbury, Oswestry and working as a domestic servant. She lived in Maesbury and Maesbury Marsh all her life with George although for some years they did live down the road in the famous St Winifred Wellhouse, Woolston (after Edward moved out). By 1911 they had moved back to Maesbury with George their younger children and her aged parents came to stay with them. She died in the Oswestry district.; [Note Record]
