Notes

1. Lived with parents at 15 Rock Road during 1901 census Lived with parents at 18 Emmet Square during 1911 census Paid for the the burial of a Thomas Doyle at Deansgrange in 1952; [Note Record]
2. Thomas was born near Booterstown, and baptised in St John the Baptist Church, Blackrock. Presume he lived at Seafort Parade in 1869 where Catherine was born. His age is reported inconsistently on census forms which also report that they had another child who died in infancy. He lived in various houses in Booterstown. He listed himself as a general labourer. We think Thomas had about 6 brothers including a John.; [Note Record]
3. 1869 - Lived in Coolmore near Brittas Bay when daughter Bridget was born Apparently a Tailor in Scratenagh/Coolmore near Arklow; [Note Record]
4. After her husband John died she lived with her Mother. After her mother died she lived with friend Bessie.; [Note Record]
5. Bridget was born in Coolmore, Co Wicklow. Seems to have lived in various housed around Booterstown She gave birth to Catherine in a house on Seafort Parade, Blackrock. Census suggests they had a third child who died at infancy. Bridget was remembered as a short lady.; [Note Record]
6. Another person named Nan Mitchell was mentioned in reference to Bridget, perhaps a mother?; [Note Record]
