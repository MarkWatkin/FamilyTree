Notes

1. Elizabeth was born in Earl Soham, Suffolk, but when she was about 4 years old, her family moved some miles away to Laxfield, Suffolk. She was the eldest of 13 children. It is here that she probably met her husband Jonathan who was born in Laxfield, 9 years her elder. They may therefore have known each other from quite a young age - their first child was born when Elizabeth was only 18. They presumably set up their first home a few miles away in Mendham as this is where their first 9 children were born. They then had another 8 children in Laxfield, so Elizabeth had 17 children in all over a period of 20 years (although at least 5 died as infants). She lived on a further 20 years so she would have seen all the surviving children to adulthood.; [Note Record]
