1. Anne was born in Maesbury. She lived at Marsh Road initially but later lived at the famous St Winifred Wellhouse in Woolston with his parents in 1901. She married Percy Harper in the second quarter 1915 at High Ercall after courting for eight years at Cotwall, High Ercall Shropshire where they worked on the farm of Mr. Bruikshaw (according to granddaughter Valerie Harper. She was later brought back to see St Winifred's Wellhouse by her son and was saddened to see it had fallen into disrepair by the local farmer. It has since been refurbished.; [Note Record]

3. Walter Harper was born in 3 Cotwall, High Ercall, Shropshire He worked as a farm boy in Shropshire. He was a WW2 Army Sgt.S.I.B (Special Investigation Branch) in Cairo, Code Breaker & Detective. Fireman & Driver + turntable ladder operator till retirement. Then he worked at Barrells the undertakers. In 1986 he dies of a stroke and heart attack while living at 10 Winterhill Rd., Cosham, Portsmouth, Hampshire, England. His ashes interred at St Michael's Churchyard High Ercall, Shropshire with his Mum & Dad, Percy & Annie.; [Note Record]
