Notes

1. Marriage witnessed by George Lewis and Annie Lewis (Edward's parents or siblings); [Note Record]
2. Edward was the love child of Charlotte and Edward, conceived a few months before they married. He worked as a guardsmen in the army. Later in life he ended up in a mental hospital in Shrewsbury; [Note Record]
3. In the 1920s Mary Helen gave birth to Gladys, with a previous partner. She lived in the home of her parents at Brynycastle cottage in Gobowen with husband William Owen after their marriage. Later on, daughter Gladys came to live with them, although the rest of the family believed Gladys to be their aunt for many years. In the 1970s, after husband William has passed on, she visited her son Edward Henry's family in Ireland and stayed for some time, after having a stroke, in Ireland. Here she taught her grandson Mark Watkin to read. She would have stayed in Ireland, except for a further stroke after she returned to England making travel difficult. Mary Helen lived her later years in homes for the elderly in the Gobowen area.; [Note Record]
4. Later in life Andrew lived in Weston Rhyn. He also worked as a truck driver with a transport company in Gobowen; [Note Record]
5. Not much is known of Gladys Maud. She was unknown to sister, Mary Helen's descendents, except for her discovery in the 1911 census; [Note Record]
