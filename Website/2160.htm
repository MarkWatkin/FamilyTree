Notes

1. Not much is know of George Bywater, except that he is listed as a farmer on son John's marriage cert. The fact that he is listed as farmer rather than labourer may suggest there was some wealth in his family. I did find an entry... Shropshire: Chirbury, Church Stretton - Parish Registers, Hereford Diocese has a George Bywater baptised on 11 Feb 1791 of John and Anne Bywater; [Note Record]
2. Born in Llanyblodwel, William's father has died before his 21st birthday He is seen again in 1851 living with mother and sister Martha in Llanyblodwel near niece Jane. In 1854 he marries Mary Williams.; [Note Record]
3. After marrying Mary Jones In Llanyblodwel in 1838 and listed as a farmer and coming from Abercynlleth, parish of Llangedwin. Like his father, the profession of farmer, rather than labourer may suggest some wealth in the family, further backed up by his marriage to Mary Jones daughter of the Jones also listed as farmers. In 1838 he has his daughter Jane with Mary, but Mary dies in 1839. By 1841 John appears to no longer be present and no record is seen of him after this as yet; [Note Record]
4. Born in Llanyblodwel, Martha's father has died before her 14th birthday. She is seen again in 1851 living with mother and brother William in Llanyblodwel near niece Jane. In 1854 she marries John Davies (Marriage cert index: 1854 Q2 11b 498A); [Note Record]
