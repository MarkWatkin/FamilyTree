Notes

1. Not much is known of David Watkin yet, except that he is listed as a farm labourer in Llansilin on son Johns marriage cert.; [Note Record]
2. John was born and raised in Llanrhaeadr-ym-Mochnant as one of 4 children of farm labourers. In 1858 after marrying Jane Bywater in Bryn, Llanyblodwel he starts a family and initially moves in with Jane's grandparents Edward and Mary Jones, where Jane had been living in Bryn Hall. Here he too worked as a farm labourer. By 1871 they had moved into their own place, Olde Bryn Cottage where they reared their children. He stayed on in Olde Bryn Cottage for the rest of his life but according to 1891 census and Roberts marriage cert in 1898 he ended us as a game keeper. Overall they had 10 children, including a Watkin Watkin :-); [Note Record]
