Gladys LEWIS, eldest child of Willam Owen WATKIN and Mary Helen LEWIS [Family], was born about 1925 in Oswestry, Shropshire, England.

Gladys was born of an earlier partner of Mary Helen and raised as an aunt with the Lewis name. Although she did come to live with her siblings a little later on after mother Mary Helen married William Owen. Gladys married in Gobowen and stayed in the village to this day.
