I have reason to believe that this Samuel and wife Susan are the same Samuel/Susan that appear in a genealogical study "William D Clutton, Genealogy and family history of William Clutton (1675-1732) of Fressingfield, Suffolk, England". In this book a Susan Clutton marries a Samuel Winter, gardener for Lord Huntingfield's estate., and they have a child Elizabeth Winter in Huntingfield (along with all the other children listed here). In this book Samuel and Susan move to Farnworth, Bolton near Manchester in 1836 to work on Thomas Barnes cotton mill and Susan dies circa 1850. I found the following evidence to tie these people in as Betsy Winter's parents: 1) Our Betsy Winter, living in Weston Rhyn, Shropshire, based on numerous census records is born in the region of 1831-1834 (inconstant ages) in Huntingfield, Suffolk (a small townland the other side of the country). 2) On her marriage cert in 1860 she lists her father as a Samuel Winter a farm bailiff (a rare enough profession among the masses) 3) In 1861 census there is a Samuel Winter b 1800 listed as a farm bailiff living only a few fields away from Betsy, in Bronygarth. This Samuel Winter says he is born in Chediston, Suffolk (the other side of the country very very near Huntingfield, Suffolk) and his wife Helena Maria b 1807 is listed as coming from Runcorn, Cheshire. 4) In 1851 census many miles away in Farnworth. Bolton. Lancashire, we have a Samuel Winter b 1798 from Chediston, Suffolk with a wife Helena Maria b 1807 coming from Runcorn, Cheshire with 2 children, Betsy Winter born 1830 in Huntingfield, Suffolk and Lucy Winter born 1831 in Huntingfield, Suffolk 5) In 1841 census still in Farnworth. Bolton. Lancashire, we have a Samuel Winter b 1800 with wife Susan b 1795 with children Samuel 1821, Joseph 1825, Mary 1825, Susan 1826, Betsy 1830, Lucy 1831 all listed as being born in different county. Perhaps a different wife in 1841? Perhaps a leap of faith? But... 6) In Bolton. Lancashire, we have a marriage record between a Samuel Winter and a Helena Maria Wylde listing the marriage as occuring in Jan-March 1845 7) In Bolton. Lancashire we have a death record for a Susan Winter in July-Sep 1844 So it looks like Helena would not have been around in 1841 and Samuel Winter COULD have been married to a Susan in 1841 who had since died (admittedly we have not got as strong a confirmation that this Samuel Winter and Susan are the same family as they do not list their birth places in 1841 census) 8) This is where we step over to the research of the William Clutton tree, which includes: Samuel Winter b 1798 marrying a Susan Clutton b 1791 in 1818 Laxfield, Suffolk (near Chediston and Huntingfield) - Samuel Winter and Susan Clutton having children Samuel 1819, Joseph 1821, Mary 1823, Elizabeth 1824 died young, Susan 1826, Charlotte 1827, Elizabeth 1829, Lucy 1831, Harriet 1834 all born in Huntingfield, Suffolk - Samuel Winter and Susan Clutton moving from Huntingfield, Suffolk in 1836 to the other side of the country Farnworth, Bolton, Lancashire It is from William Clutton's book from which the above ancestry all flows. So... Samuel is born in Chediston, Suffolk and marries Susan Clutton in Laxfield and works as a gardener on Lord Huntingfield's estate in Suffolk where their family is born. In 1836 he moves to Farnworth in Lancashire to work in Thomas Barnes cotton mill. In 1844 his wife Susan dies and in 1845 he remarries Helena Maria Wylde. In 1851 he is listed as a farmers servant living with family at 112 Green Bank, Farnsworth, Lancashire with 2nd wife Helena Maria and the kids, Betsy and Lucy. On Betsy marriage cert he is listed as a farm bailiff an by 1861 he is living with Helena Maria in Bronygarth, Shropshire a few fields away from Betsy and James farm. He died in Shropshire.; [Note Record]
2. Elizabeth Winter (signed her name Betsy on all census forms and certs). She was born in Huntingfield, Halesworth in Suffolk of parents Susan and Samuel Winter. I have reason to believe that this Susan is the same Susan that appears in a genealogical study "William D Clutton, Genealogy and family history of William Clutton (1675-1732) of Fressingfield, Suffolk, England". In this book a Susan Clutton marries a Samuel Winter, gardener for Lord Huntingfields estate., and they have a child Elizabeth Winter in Huntingfield. In this book Samuel and Susan move to Farnworth, Bolton near Manchester in 1836. to work on Thomas Barnes cotton mill and Susan dies in 1850. I found an 1851 census record for Farnworth, which lists a Samuel from Chediston near Huntingfield, Suffolk, with a wife Helena Maria (presumably second wife ). They have with them a child Betsy Winter also from Huntingfield. I also found an 1861 census record which puts the same Samuel Winter and Helena Maria in Bronygarth only a few hundred yards from Betsy Winter and James Richardsons farm. Hence my belief that our Betsy Winter's parents are indeed Samuel Winter and Susan Clutton, from the William Clutton book from which the above ancestry all flows. Betsy Winter ends up in Shropshire, possibly due to the fact that her father's new wife comes from nearby Cheshire. She marries James Richardson in 1860. They farm at Trehowel Farm of 30 acres, then Tyn-y-Celyn farm of 117 acres, and employerd servants and labourers. They then moved to another farm, and their 2 sons took over Trehowel and Tyn-y- Celyn both employing staff. Is has been reported that they "disowned" their youngest, Charlotte, when she became pregnant and later married Edward Lewis (who she may have met at the blacksmth in neighbouring St Martins where he was working as an apprentice). Betsy died before her husband at Pentre Bronygarth, Weston Rhyn leaving £136 16s 2d to her husband James.; [Note Record]
