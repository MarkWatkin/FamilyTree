John Winter RICHARDSON, eldest son of James RICHARDSON and Betsy WINTER [Family], was born on 02/03/1867 in Trehowel, St Martins, Oswestry, Shropshire, England. John died in 1955 aged about 88.

He resided in 1867 in Trehowel, St Martins, Oswestry, Shropshire, England. He resided in 1881 in Tyn-Y-Celyn, Weston Rhyn, Shropshire, Ireland. In 1901 John resided in Trehowel, St Martins, Oswestry, Shropshire, England. He was a Farmer at Trehowel Farm in 1901. John was a Farmer at Trehowel Farm in 1911. John resided in 1911 in Trehowel, St Martins, Oswestry, Shropshire, England. In 1912 he resided in Trehowel, St Martins, Oswestry, Shropshire, England. In 1912 John was a Farmer at Trehowel.

John Winter Richardson was born at the family farm, Trehowel in Weston Rhyn/St Martins. By the time he was 14, in 1881, his family moved to the larger Tyn-y-Celyn farm. He married Mary just before the turn of the century and by 1901 he was back managing Trehowel farm himself where he had 2 servants. He presumably spent some time here as the 1911 census and the 1912 Kelly's directory of businesses both place him as farmer at Trehowel. It is unclear whether or not he and the other Richardson's owned these lands or were tenant farmers.
