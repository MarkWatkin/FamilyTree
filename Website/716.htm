Cornelius RODERICK1, only child of William RODERICK and Gwen WILLIAMS, was born on 20/03/1730 in Oswestry Hospital, Shropshire, England. Cornelius died on 10/01/1805 in Maesbury, Oswestry, Shropshire, England aged 74.

Elizabeth WILLIAMS2.

On 30/07/1768 Cornelius married Elizabeth. 2 sons and 2 daughters given.

William RODERICK was born on 20/03/1775 in Oswestry, Shropshire, England.
