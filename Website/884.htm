Text In the 1920s Mary Helen gave birth to Gladys, with a previous partner. She lived in the home of her parents at Brynycastle cottage in Gobowen with husband William Owen after their marriage. Later on, daughter Gladys came to live with them, although the rest of the family believed Gladys to be their aunt for many years. In the 1970s, after husband William has passed on, she visited her son Edward Henry's family in Ireland and stayed for some time, after having a stroke, in Ireland. Here she taught her grandson Mark Watkin to read.
She would have stayed in Ireland, except for a further stroke after she returned to England making travel difficult. Mary Helen lived her later years in homes for the elderly in the Gobowen area.
