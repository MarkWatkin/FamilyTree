George BYWATER. George died before 1841.

He was a Farmer in 1838 in Abercynlleth, Llangedwin, Denbighshire, Wales.

Not much is know of George Bywater, except that he is listed as a farmer on son John's marriage cert. The fact that he is listed as farmer rather than labourer may suggest there was some wealth in his family. I did find an entry...
