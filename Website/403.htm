Maria REYNOLDS, only child of James REYNOLDS and Elizabeth MILLS [Family], was born between 04/1819 and 1820 in Worksop, Nottinghamshire, England.

Before 28/11/1840 Maria resided in Low Town, Worksop, Nottinghamshire, England. She resided on 30/03/1851 in Bilby, Barnby Moor, Blythe, East Retford, Nottinghamshire. Maria resided on 07/04/1861 in Bilby, Barnby Moor, Blythe, East Retford, Nottinghamshire. She resided on 02/04/1871 in Worksop, Nottinghamshire, England. She resided on 03/04/1881 in 5 Bracebridge, Worksop, Nottinghamshire, England. On 05/04/1891 Maria resided in 5 Bracebridge, Worksop, Nottinghamshire, England.

Prior to getting married Maria lived in Low Town, Worksop, Nottinghamshire and was listed as a servant. After marrying William Hallam from nearby Bilby they moved between Worksop and Bilby and raised a family. In 1871 they appear to be living with or next door to the Mallender family (where John Mallender is also from Barnby and Mary Mallender is also from Worksop). By 1881 they have moved to 5 Bracebridge, with Maria and youngest son Walter. By 1891 (still at 5 Bracebridge) Walters has left home but now they have granddaughter Maria Mallender living with them.
