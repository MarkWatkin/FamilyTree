Notes

1. Jonathan was born in Laxfield, Suffolk. His wife-to-be moved there at the age of 4, so they may have met quite young - their first child was born when Elizabeth was only 18. They presumably set up their first home a few miles away in Mendham as this is where their first 9 children were born. They then had another 8 children in Laxfield, 17 children in all over a period of 20 years (although at least 5 died as infants). He lived on a further 30 years so would have seen all the surviving children to adulthood. Jonathan died on Lord Huntingfield's estate at the home of his daughter Susan, where Susan's husband worked.; [Note Record]
2. I have reason to believe that this Samuel and wife Susan are the same Samuel/Susan that appear in a genealogical study "William D Clutton, Genealogy and family history of William Clutton (1675-1732) of Fressingfield, Suffolk, England". In this book a Susan Clutton marries a Samuel Winter, gardener for Lord Huntingfield's estate., and they have a child Elizabeth Winter in Huntingfield (along with all the other children listed here). In this book Samuel and Susan move to Farnworth, Bolton near Manchester in 1836 to work on Thomas Barnes cotton mill and Susan dies circa 1850. I found the following evidence to tie these people in as Betsy Winter's parents: 1) Our Betsy Winter, living in Weston Rhyn, Shropshire, based on numerous census records is born in the region of 1831-1834 (inconstant ages) in Huntingfield, Suffolk (a small townland the other side of the country). 2) On her marriage cert in 1860 she lists her father as a Samuel Winter a farm bailiff (a rare enough profession among the masses) 3) In 1861 census there is a Samuel Winter b 1800 listed as a farm bailiff living only a few fields away from Betsy, in Bronygarth. This Samuel Winter says he is born in Chediston, Suffolk (the other side of the country very very near Huntingfield, Suffolk) and his wife Helena Maria b 1807 is listed as coming from Runcorn, Cheshire. 4) In 1851 census many miles away in Farnworth. Bolton. Lancashire, we have a Samuel Winter b 1798 from Chediston, Suffolk with a wife Helena Maria b 1807 coming from Runcorn, Cheshire with 2 children, Betsy Winter born 1830 in Huntingfield, Suffolk and Lucy Winter born 1831 in Huntingfield, Suffolk 5) In 1841 census still in Farnworth. Bolton. Lancashire, we have a Samuel Winter b 1800 with wife Susan b 1795 with children Samuel 1821, Joseph 1825, Mary 1825, Susan 1826, Betsy 1830, Lucy 1831 all listed as being born in different county. Perhaps a different wife in 1841? Perhaps a leap of faith? But... 6) In Bolton. Lancashire, we have a marriage record between a Samuel Winter and a Helena Maria Wylde listing the marriage as occuring in Jan-March 1845 7) In Bolton. Lancashire we have a death record for a Susan Winter in July-Sep 1844 So it looks like Helena would not have been around in 1841 and Samuel Winter COULD have been married to a Susan in 1841 who had since died (admittedly we have not got as strong a confirmation that this Samuel Winter and Susan are the same family as they do not list their birth places in 1841 census) 8) This is where we step over to the research of the William Clutton tree, which includes: Samuel Winter b 1798 marrying a Susan Clutton b 1791 in 1818 Laxfield, Suffolk (near Chediston and Huntingfield) - Samuel Winter and Susan Clutton having children Samuel 1819, Joseph 1821, Mary 1823, Elizabeth 1824 died young, Susan 1826, Charlotte 1827, Elizabeth 1829, Lucy 1831, Harriet 1834 all born in Huntingfield, Suffolk - Samuel Winter and Susan Clutton moving from Huntingfield, Suffolk in 1836 to the other side of the country Farnworth, Bolton, Lancashire It is from William Clutton's book from which the above ancestry all flows. So... Susan is born in Laxfield Suffolk and marries Samuel Winter in Laxfield where they live on Lord Huntingfield's estate in Suffolk where their family is born. In 1836 they move to Farnworth in Lancashire to work in Thomas Barnes cotton mill. Susan dies in 1844 in Farnworth.; [Note Record]
