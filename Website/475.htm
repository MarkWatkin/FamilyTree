Thomas WARNER, only child of Nicholas WARNER [Family], was born about 1430 in Fressingfield, Suffolk, England.

Thomas grew up in Fressingfield and spent some adult years in nearby Wingfield. Deeds give Wingfield as his residence in 1452 and 1478, Fressingfield in 1502 and 1503. A Thomas Warner of Wingfield prepared his will 24 Feb 1504, naming daughter Alice and son John. This Thomas could be Johane's father, if he omitted her from his will because she had already received her portion although further evidence is, however, required.
