Notes

1. Edward Henry, known as Henry in the UK and Harry in Ireland was born in Gobowen, Oswestry. As a young man he spent some time in the RAF and won scholarships to study at University, but decided against such careers. He met his wife Veronica on a tour or Europe in 1954 and moved to Ireland where he raised a large family, working various careers, including managing his own contract cleaning company. He had a passion for carpentry, building dolls houses and was a keen artist specialising in pencil and pen and ink drawings. He retired to West Cork with Veronica and his daughter Judith's family, where he spends much time in the garden.; [Note Record]
2. Veronica grew up in Booterstown on the Rock Road with her 2 sisters. She enjoyed long cycles with her father Arthur around Wicklow and was a great fan of opera music. She met her husband Harry on European tour with her sister in 1954. She settled in Dublin where she raised a family of 8 children with Harry (6 of whom were born at home). She retired with Harry to West Cork with Harry and her daughter, Judith's family, where she enjoys the country life in the village of Skibbereen.; [Note Record]
