Notes

1. Alice was born out of Wedlock while Ann still living with parents . In 1871 she is living with her grandparents after Ann has married and moved on. There is no record of her after this.; [Note Record]
2. George Lewis was born in Maesbury. Prior to his marriage in 1873 he is listed as a farm labourer still in Maesbury. He lived in Maesbury and Maesbury Marsh all his life although for some years he did live down the road in the famous St Winifred's Wellhouse, Woolston (after Edward moved out). By 1911 he had moved back to Maesbury with his wife younger children and his aged parents in law.; [Note Record]
3. David was born in Welshpool. He presumably married some time around 1837 although cannot find a marriage cert. He moved to West Felton where is wife Mary came from. He works all his life as an agricultural labourer around Maesbury area until he moved to Oswestry where he lived with his grand daughter Fanny Morgan and a grandson Thomas Curry.; [Note Record]
4. Andrew Currie was born in Stewarton, East Ayrshire in Scotland where he was christened some months later. He moved to Daventry in Northamptonshire in the 1840s where he met his future wife Rebecca Powell Adams. He worked as various jobs over his life (Tea trader, rag and bone man, pedlar, insurance agent). By 1861 he had moved to Shropshire with his family where he lived out the rest of his life. In his 80s he and his wife moved in with his daughter Ann with her husband George Lewis an their children in Munslow Cottages, Maesbury Marsh.; [Note Record]
5. Found evidence of Scottish Census for John Currie and his family in 1841, 1851 and 1861; [Note Record]
6. Ann was born in Daventry, Northamptonshire, where her mother grew up and her Scottish father moved to. Prior to her marriage in 1873 she is living at Bromwick Park, Maesbury, Oswestry and working as a domestic servant. She lived in Maesbury and Maesbury Marsh all her life with George although for some years they did live down the road in the famous St Winifred Wellhouse, Woolston (after Edward moved out). By 1911 they had moved back to Maesbury with George their younger children and her aged parents came to stay with them. She died in the Oswestry district.; [Note Record]
7. Rebecca Powell Adams was born in Floore/Flore Northamptonshire and was christened in Ravensthorpe. She took the name of her 2 parents. By 1841 she is living with a Frances and Ann Hickerman at Ravensthorpe, Northamptonshire with their son Edward and a William Adams b 1827 presumably her brother. She is listed as a visitor but is also present at the same address 10 years later. In 1851 she is listed as an annuitant, which makes me believe she in now an orphan and possibly of wealthier parents? She meets the Scottish Andrew Currie then living in Daventry and marries in 1852. By 1861 the family have relocated to Shropshire she lives out her days in the Maesbury area. In her 80s she and Andrew moved in with his daughter Ann with her husband George Lewis an their children in Munslow Cottages, Maesbury Marsh.; [Note Record]
8. Found evidence of Scottish Census for John Currie and his family in 1841, 1851 and 1861; [Note Record]
9. Mary was born in West Felton in 1815/1816. (Tracing her family was a challenge largely achieved by cousin Valerie Harper based on the following evidence. Her marriage cert cites her father as blacksmith, William Roderick. Her marriage was witnessed by a Hannah Roderick. A widowed midwife, Martha Roderick ended up living next door to her in 1851, the same profession as Mary in later life. We also find a William Roderick and Martha Roderick having 6 other children in West Felton with a gap around the years of Mary's birth. Furthermore this William Roderick has a census record citing him as blacksmith also. Furthermore all the other names of this family show up again in Mary children. This all leads us to believe her parents were indeed this same William Roderick and Martha Roderick for whom we have christening notices for many other children but sadly not including Mary) On 20 Mar 1836 she has an illegit son William Roderick with father Thomas Woollam. After this in 1837 she marries local labourer David Lewis while living in Knockin and working as a servant. They marry at Knockin parish church. They lived mostly in the Maesbury area until moving to Oswestry in 1881 where they lived with her grand daughter Fanny Morgan and a grandson Thomas Currie. Here Mary worked as a midwife. By 1891 she is widowed and living at Dodd's Cottages as a "Monthly Nurse" (a minder post childbirth).; [Note Record]
