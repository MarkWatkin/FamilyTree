Notes

1. George Lewis was born in Maesbury. Prior to his marriage in 1873 he is listed as a farm labourer still in Maesbury. He lived in Maesbury and Maesbury Marsh all his life although for some years he did live down the road in the famous St Winifred's Wellhouse, Woolston (after Edward moved out). By 1911 he had moved back to Maesbury with his wife younger children and his aged parents in law.; [Note Record]
2. Anne was born in Maesbury. She lived at Marsh Road initially but later lived at the famous St Winifred Wellhouse in Woolston with his parents in 1901. She married Percy Harper in the second quarter 1915 at High Ercall after courting for eight years at Cotwall, High Ercall Shropshire where they worked on the farm of Mr. Bruikshaw (according to granddaughter Valerie Harper. She was later brought back to see St Winifred's Wellhouse by her son and was saddened to see it had fallen into disrepair by the local farmer. It has since been refurbished.; [Note Record]
3. Ann was born in Daventry, Northamptonshire, where her mother grew up and her Scottish father moved to. Prior to her marriage in 1873 she is living at Bromwick Park, Maesbury, Oswestry and working as a domestic servant. She lived in Maesbury and Maesbury Marsh all her life with George although for some years they did live down the road in the famous St Winifred Wellhouse, Woolston (after Edward moved out). By 1911 they had moved back to Maesbury with George their younger children and her aged parents came to stay with them. She died in the Oswestry district.; [Note Record]
