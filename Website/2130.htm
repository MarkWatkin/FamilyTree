5. Andrew Currie was born in Stewarton, East Ayrshire in Scotland where he was christened some months later. He moved to Daventry in Northamptonshire in the 1840s where he met his future wife Rebecca Powell Adams. He worked as various jobs over his life (Tea trader, rag and bone man, pedlar, insurance agent). By 1861 he had moved to Shropshire with his family where he lived out the rest of his life. In his 80s he and his wife moved in with his daughter Ann with her husband George Lewis an their children in Munslow Cottages, Maesbury Marsh.; [Note Record]
6. Rebecca Powell Adams was born in Floore/Flore Northamptonshire and was christened in Ravensthorpe. She took the name of her 2 parents. By 1841 she is living with a Frances and Ann Hickerman at Ravensthorpe, Northamptonshire with their son Edward and a William Adams b 1827 presumably her brother. She is listed as a visitor but is also present at the same address 10 years later. In 1851 she is listed as an annuitant, which makes me believe she in now an orphan and possibly of wealthier parents? She meets the Scottish Andrew Currie then living in Daventry and marries in 1852. By 1861 the family have relocated to Shropshire she lives out her days in the Maesbury area. In her 80s she and Andrew moved in with his daughter Ann with her husband George Lewis an their children in Munslow Cottages, Maesbury Marsh.; [Note Record]
