Mary was born in West Felton in 1815/1816. (Tracing her family was a challenge largely achieved by cousin Valerie Harper based on the following evidence. Her marriage cert cites her father as blacksmith, William Roderick. Her marriage was witnessed by a Hannah Roderick. A widowed midwife, Martha Roderick ended up living next door to her in 1851, the same profession as Mary in later life. We also find a William Roderick and Martha Roderick having 6 other children in West Felton with a gap around the years of Mary's birth. Furthermore this William Roderick has a census record citing him as blacksmith also. Furthermore all the other names of this family show up again in Mary children. This all leads us to believe her parents were indeed this same William Roderick and Martha Roderick for whom we have christening notices for many other children but sadly not including Mary) On 20 Mar 1836 she has an illegit son William Roderick with father Thomas Woollam. After this in 1837 she marries local labourer David Lewis while living in Knockin and working as a servant. They marry at Knockin parish church. They lived mostly in the Maesbury area until moving to Oswestry in 1881 where they lived with her grand daughter Fanny Morgan and a grandson Thomas Currie. Here Mary worked as a midwife. By 1891 she is widowed and living at Dodd's Cottages as a "Monthly Nurse" (a minder post childbirth).

8 sons and 3 daughters listed.
