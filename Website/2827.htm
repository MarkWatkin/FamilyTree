Notes

1. Alice was born out of Wedlock while Ann still living with parents . In 1871 she is living with her grandparents after Ann has married and moved on. There is no record of her after this.; [Note Record]
2. Marriage it witnessed by Ann's sister Mary Currie and George's brother Edward Lewis; [Note Record]
3. Born and raised in Maesbury in Shropshire. Edward left home as a teenager and I believe I have found him aged 17 in the 1891 census, working as an apprentice blacksmith and living with a blacksmith family of John Thomas next to the vicarage and Trevor Cottage in St Martins. This is presumably where he met his wife Charlotte Richardson who grew up on a farm in the neighbouring village of Weston Rhyn. Edward and Charlotte marry in late 1899 in the Oswestry registry office (about 4 months pregnant with their first child Edward Donald). At the time of the marriage they are both living in Maesbury (Marsh) and the marriage is witnessed by Edward's parents. This, and word of mouth evidence suggests that Charlotte's match and/or premarital pregnancy caused friction between her and family and she was "disowned". Although in later life Charlotte's grandchildren did visit the Richardson's farm so the "disowning" was not entire. After the marriage by 1901, they are living in 40 New Hall View, Ruabon with new baby Edward Donald and Edward is employed as Railway platelayer (These houses are gone, replaced by St Michael's Close) A year later, Mary Helen is born at Bringewood, Queen Street, Ruabon and Edward is still listed as a platelayer, as he is in 1911 by which time they have moved to 1 Bryn Castle Terrace, Gobowen, just opposite the school. In 1914 Edward joins the army, although I have found no further information about this. At some point between 1918 and 1927 they moved into Brynycastle Cottage, Gobowen and ultimately passed this house onto daughter Mary Helen, who raised her own family there with William Owen. In 1927, Charlotte dies and for some unknown reason leaves some substantial cash in her will to her brother who at this stage is a farm owner. Edward stays on at Brynycastle Cottage, Gobowen after William Owen and Mary Helen started their family there and at one point even shared a room and bed with his grandson Edward Henry.; [Note Record]
4. Cornelius was born in the Oswestry area. While living with his parents in 1881, no record of him has been found after this.; [Note Record]
5. William was born in the Oswestry area. While living with his parents in 1881, no record of him has been found after this.; [Note Record]
6. David was born in Maesbury in 1883 and in 1891 was living at Marsh road. In 1901 He was living with his family at the famous St Winifred Wellhouse in Woolston. According to Valerie Harper his grand niece, he moved to Canada and became a rancher.; [Note Record]
7. Anne was born in Maesbury. She lived at Marsh Road initially but later lived at the famous St Winifred Wellhouse in Woolston with his parents in 1901. She married Percy Harper in the second quarter 1915 at High Ercall after courting for eight years at Cotwall, High Ercall Shropshire where they worked on the farm of Mr. Bruikshaw (according to granddaughter Valerie Harper. She was later brought back to see St Winifred's Wellhouse by her son and was saddened to see it had fallen into disrepair by the local farmer. It has since been refurbished.; [Note Record]
8. George was born in Maesbury and lived there in 1891 but later lived at the famous St Winifred Wellhouse in Woolston with his parents in 1901.; [Note Record]
9. John lived at the famous St Winifred Wellhouse in Woolston with his parents in 1901.; [Note Record]
10. John lived at the famous St Winifred Wellhouse in Woolston with his parents in 1901.; [Note Record]
11. Mary was known as Polly. She lives at the famous St Winifred Wellhouse in Woolston with her parents. She may have had a child called Reg but I have found no record of this. A cousin Valerie Harper Phillips suggests that she married a Walter Harper (1896-1974) and had children Walter (1921-1983) and Alice (1925-1993).; [Note Record]
